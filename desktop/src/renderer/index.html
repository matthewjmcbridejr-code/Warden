    Welcome to Warden AI Desk
    
      
    
    
    
    
    Warden is your personal AI workspace that links sandboxed web platforms with safe, isolated build missions. Let's set up your first success.
    
    
    
      
    01Chat accounts
    Sign in to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok in Chat. Warden browser profiles keep your web sessions isolated.
    
      
    02Build accounts
    Connect official local clients (Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Grok) with your existing subscription.
    
      
    03Safe missions
    Warden runs agent missions in an isolated worktree. You inspect changes, checks, and proof before applying.
